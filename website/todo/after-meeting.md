# After one meeting

Three details, all in `BUSINESS` in `site/src/consts.ts`. Each shows greyed on the pages until it's filled in.

- [ ] **Address** (`address`): on the Terms, Privacy and Refunds pages, and on receipts.
- [ ] **Grievance officer** (`grievanceOfficer`): a name and an email, on the Privacy page. India's data protection law
  asks for one.
- [ ] **City for disputes** (`courts`): on the Terms page.

Then change `TERMS_VERSION` in the same file to that day's date.
